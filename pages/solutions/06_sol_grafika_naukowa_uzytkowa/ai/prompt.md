# Prompt ilustracji

Wygenerowano jedną wersję w narzędziu ImageGen (wbudowanym generatorze obrazów).

~~~text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: horizontal conceptual illustration for a university teaching exercise about scientific graphics
Primary request: illustrate the idea of moving from raw observations to an organized understanding
Scene/backdrop: clean warm off-white background
Subject: on the left a loose cluster of abstract colored paper dots and small observation marks; through the center they become neatly grouped geometric shapes; on the right a simple open notebook with a subtle lightbulb motif, representing interpretation
Style/medium: sophisticated flat editorial illustration with a slight paper texture, hand-crafted feel, restrained blue, teal, and warm orange palette
Composition/framing: wide horizontal composition, generous breathing room, visually balanced
Constraints: clearly an illustration, not a real chart or scientific result; no text, no numbers, no axes, no logos, no watermarks
~~~
